What the Project Does

What the Project Does

An AI-powered rural health kiosk that expands healthcare access in underserved communities, letting patients interact with a virtual assistant via voice and text.

Built on Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG), multimodal AI, and remote diagnostics, it supports symptom assessment, triage, and personalized care. As part of the CoDiRA initiative, it emphasizes culturally sensitive, multilingual, and community-driven delivery.

Why This Matters?

Why This Matters?

  • Rural and underserved populations face limited access to healthcare
  • Shortage of medical professionals in remote areas
  • Delayed diagnosis leads to preventable complications
  • Existing chatbots lack realism and bedside manner
Our Solution

Our Solution

A smart AI-powered healthcare kiosk that provides accessible medical support through voice, text, and empathetic interaction. Designed for underserved populations, it pairs advanced tech with multilingual support, virtual education and community collaboration.

What Makes It Unique?

What Makes It Unique?

  • Fine-tuned MedGemma model for bedside manner
  • Real-time avatar interaction
  • Voice-to-voice AI communication
  • Retrieval-Augmented Generation for medical context
  • Designed for training and communication practice
